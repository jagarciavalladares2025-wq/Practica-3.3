Artist Profiles

Dua Lipa • Olivia Rodrigo • Belinda

Dua Lipa

The Disco-Pop Queen

Dua Lipa is a British singer and model of Albanian and Kosovar descent. Born in London, she signed with Warner Records in 2014 and became a definitive global pioneer of the modern disco-pop era.

  • 3 Grammy Awards won to date.
  • Self-titled debut album released in 2017.
  • Global fashion icon and international activist.

Olivia Rodrigo

The Voice of Gen Z

Olivia Rodrigo is an American singer-songwriter and actress who gained initial fame on Disney programs. She completely redefined teenage emotional expression in modern pop music.

  • Massive record-breaking success with her single "Drivers License".
  • Winner of 3 Grammy Awards with her debut album 'Sour'.
  • Known worldwide for her honest and highly confessional lyrics.
  • Consistently leads global music streaming charts in 2024.

Belinda

The Legend of Latin Pop

Belinda Peregrín, widely recognized as the "Princess of Latin Pop", is a Spanish-born Mexican actress and singer. She possesses an expansive and highly versatile musical trajectory.

  • Immigrated with her family to Mexico in 1993.
  • Began her highly successful career as a child actress in 2000.
  • Millions of albums sold across Latin America.
  • Artistic versatility blending pop, rock, and electronic beats.
